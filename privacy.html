Privacy

Short version: we collect as little as possible, we never sell it, and anything we build for you lives in your own accounts.

Visiting this website

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service. It does not use cookies and does not store personal information. We see totals like "how many people visited" and "how many downloaded the free tracker," plus general details such as the page, the referring site, browser type and country. We can't tell who you are from it.

Don't want to be counted at all? Add #toggle-goatcounter to the end of this page's address once, and your browser will stop being counted.

When you email or call us

If you email, call, or text us (including for the free checkup), we use what you send only to answer you and, if you want, to quote and do the work. We keep it in our email and our customer list. We don't sell or share it with anyone for marketing.

Want us to delete what you sent? Email marcus@stonerivercoast.com and we will.

Our emails

If we email you about our services, every email includes our mailing address and an easy way to stop. Reply "stop" and you won't hear from us again.

Work we do for you

Trackers, guides and listings we set up for clients are built in the client's own accounts. We don't keep copies of client data in our accounts, and we never ask for banking or payment passwords.
