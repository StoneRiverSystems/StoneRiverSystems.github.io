How a setup comes together

An example for a fictional 3-person lawn care company. Names and numbers are illustrative, not client results.

  1. The problem

    Jobs were tracked in a notebook and text messages, and invoicing happened at the end of the week.

  2. What we built

    One Google Sheet in the owner's account with job statuses (quoted, scheduled, done, billed, paid) and an outstanding-balance view.

  3. What they got

    Current jobs entered, a 30-minute walkthrough for the owner and crew lead, and a one-page reference guide.

  4. Every week

    The crew lead updates jobs from a phone. Each Monday, the owner bills completed work and follows up on anything past 30 days.
