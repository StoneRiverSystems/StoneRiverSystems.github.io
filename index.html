Sample setup · made-up business

How a setup like this comes together

An example of our process for a fictional 3-person lawn crew. The business, names and numbers are made up to show the work. They are not real client results.

  1. The problem

    Jobs lived in a notebook and text messages. The owner billed from memory at the end of the week, and some finished jobs never got invoiced.

  2. What we built

    One Google Sheet in the owner's account: a job list with simple statuses (quoted, scheduled, done, billed, paid) and a "who owes me" view sorted by how long each invoice has been out.

  3. What they got

    Their current jobs entered for them, a 30-minute walkthrough for the owner and crew lead, and a one-page how-to taped inside the truck.

  4. Every week

    The crew lead marks jobs done from a phone. Monday morning, the owner opens one view, bills anything marked done and calls anyone past 30 days.
